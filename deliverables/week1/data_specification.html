Data Specification: Chooser Option Pricing with Real-World Data

Aiden LeongQuantitative Research & Trading20 November 2026Version 1.0
This document defines the data the project will collect, where each item comes from, how it is stored, and how it maps onto the inputs of the Black-Scholes-Merton (BSM) chooser option model in Huang, Wang & Wan (2021), Exploration of JPMorgan Chooser Option Pricing. It covers three data families required by the brief (financial, macroeconomic, sentiment) plus the option-price data needed for Week 4 benchmarking. Items marked Collected are in the Week 1 raw dataset; items marked Planned are scoped here and collected in later weeks.

1. Scope and conventions

ItemSpecification
Underlying assetJPMorgan Chase & Co. common stock, ticker JPM (NYSE)

Primary frequencyDaily (end of day). Monthly macro series kept at native frequency; aligned in Week 2.
CalendarNYSE trading days define the master calendar. Rates follow the US bond-market calendar, so they are blank on a few days when stocks trade (e.g. Columbus Day, Veterans Day).
UnitsPrices in USD per share; rates and yields in percent per year, as published (convert to decimals at model time); VIX in index points (annualized implied vol, %).
Time zoneUS/Eastern trading dates; timestamps stored as plain dates (no time of day).
Raw-data ruleRaw files are saved exactly as received: no filling, interpolation or outlier removal. Cleaning is a Week 2 task, so every transformation stays traceable.

3.2 Volatility data: VIX (Yahoo Finance ^VIX; FRED VIXCLS)

File data/raw/yahoo_vix_daily.csv: the same columns as JPM; only close is meaningful (VIX has no volume or dividends). FRED VIXCLS is collected as an independent check: the two sources agree to within 0.01 on 1,759 of 1,761 days (the largest gap is 0.11).

4. Mapping the paper's parameters to data

Table 2 of Huang et al. (2021) fixes seven inputs. The table shows where each comes from in our dataset and what the raw data gives on the paper's date, 23 August 2021.

ParameterPaperOur source and methodOur value
Stock price S0$156.70Yahoo JPM unadjusted close on 2021-08-23$156.70
Volatility σ28.2%Std. dev. of daily log returns, 24 Aug 2020 – 23 Aug 2021 (252 returns), × √25227.2%
Dividend yield q2.33%Trailing 12-month dividends ($3.60) ÷ S02.30%
Risk-free rate r0.15%FRED DGS1 (1-year) on 2021-08-23; DTB3 = 0.05%, DGS6MO = 0.06%, DGS2 = 0.23%0.07%
Strike K$150Contract term; set in the Week 3 configuration filen/a
Decision date T10.5 yContract termn/a
Expiry T21 yContract termn/a
Reproducibility notes. S0 matches exactly. With 252-day annualization and a one-year window, σ comes out between 26.8% and 27.7% (log or simple returns, adjusted or unadjusted prices, sample or population standard deviation, window starts 1 Jul – 1 Sep 2020). None of the 272 conventions we tested gives 28.2% (see the Week 1 lab report, §4.4). The paper's r = 0.15% matches no single Treasury tenor on that date: it lies between the 1-year (0.07%) and 2-year (0.23%) yields. Week 3 will run the model with both the paper's values and our data-derived values and report the difference.

6. Option price data for benchmarking (Planned, Week 4)

Risk flagged now. The brief and the paper refer to "CME" option prices. JPM single-stock options are listed on US equity options exchanges (Cboe and others) and cleared by the OCC; CME Group mainly lists futures and options on futures. Historical end-of-day JPM option prices for 2018–2024 are sold by commercial vendors (e.g. Cboe DataShop, OptionMetrics via WRDS). yfinance only returns the current option chain, not history.

Options: (a) use institutional or university access to OptionMetrics or another vendor if available; (b) buy a limited Cboe DataShop extract; (c) start saving daily yfinance chain snapshots now with a scheduled GitHub Action. That gives forward-collected data only, not 2018–2024. (d) If no traded chooser prices exist, which is likely because chooser options trade over the counter, benchmark against the replicating portfolio of listed European calls and puts implied by Rubinstein (1991). A decision on (a)–(d) is needed before Week 4.

7. Storage, naming and reproducibility

  • Layout. data/raw/ holds the immutable source files (committed to Git; about 0.6 MB in total). data/cache/ holds raw Alpha Vantage JSON responses (gitignored) to save daily quota. Week 2 will add data/processed/ for cleaned files.
  • Names. <source>_<dataset>_<frequency>.csv, e.g. yahoo_jpm_daily.csv, fred_daily.csv.
  • Manifest. Each pull writes data/raw/manifest.json with the UTC timestamp, the window, row counts and every validation result.
  • One command. python -m src.pull_all tests all three connections, pulls everything and validates it. All configuration lives in src/data_sources/config.py.
  • Secrets. API keys live only in a local .env file (gitignored; the repository is public). .env.example documents the variable names.
